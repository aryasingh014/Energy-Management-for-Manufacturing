## ⚡ Overview
**EnerOps** is an enterprise-grade Energy Management & Operations Intelligence Platform built for manufacturing facilities, assembly plants, and multi-site industrial complexes. It combines real-time multi-utility telemetry (electricity, gas, compressed air, steam, water) with production metrics, machine health monitoring, AI anomaly detection, and ESG carbon reporting.

## 🚀 Key Modules
1. **Real-time Operations Dashboard**: Sub-meter monitoring, peak demand gauges, and diurnal baseline tracking.
2. **Machine Telemetry & Health**: Machine load %, temperature, vibration, and OEE-energy correlation.
3. **AI Copilot & Diagnostics**: Natural language querying, automatic anomaly detection, and predictive load forecasting.
4. **Renewable & Microgrid Integration**: Solar PV yield, Battery Storage (BESS) charge status, and avoided carbon metrics.
5. **Dynamic Tariff & Cost Management**: Time-of-Day (ToD) tariff analysis, peak penalty prevention, and what-if savings simulation.
6. **ESG & ISO 50001 Reporting**: Automated Scope 1 & 2 carbon accounting with PDF/CSV export.
